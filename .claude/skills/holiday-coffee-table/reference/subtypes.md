# Kinds of trip: what each must cover

Version 1, 7 November 2026, rebuilt from the family's v15 trip page. Every trip page starts from the
same kit, the same quality bar (SKILL.md) and the list "Every trip" below. The other lists add what
each kind of trip needs; a trip can mix kinds, so use every list that applies. Each item that states
a fact needs a source on the page.

## Every trip (the v15 standard)

- **Food, snacks and shopping first** (they matter most to this family)
  - [ ] Every meal (breakfast, lunch, dinner) has four choices open on that date, the planned place counted.
  - [ ] Each day has Taste of the day and Shop the day, with that day's shop trading hours.
  - [ ] Each area has its signature tastes, sweet and snack stops, markets on the family's days,
        shopping, and what to bring home; the trading-hours table covers every day, holidays included.
  - [ ] Awards and guide listings (with their source) instead of star ratings.
- **Places**
  - [ ] Every stop: why it is there, 2 to 4 credited photos, official links (website, address on
        Google Maps, phone, hours with their source), suitability marks, and "if it's closed or
        crowded" alternatives nearby.
  - [ ] The venue's own Instagram, TikTok or Facebook only (from its own website or listing); no
        brand or chain accounts; no age-restricted accounts.
  - [ ] One photo spot per place, only where an official source names one.
  - [ ] Places of worship near each base, with service times.
- **Near here, for every area**
  - [ ] Two hospitals, at least one with a full 24-hour emergency department (from its own site or
        the health department), and the nearest airport with scheduled flights.
  - [ ] Two police stations (24-hour counter only from an official source) and at least two clinics
        with their hours on the dates, holidays especially.
  - [ ] A supermarket, a store open 24 hours or late (the local chain for that state or city) and a
        pharmacy, with their hours on the dates.
  - [ ] One or two parks, one or two zoos, wildlife parks or aquariums (or the nearest, with its
        distance), and one or two attractions people built.
  - [ ] Each with its distance from the area's anchor and how it was worked out.
- **Days**
  - [ ] Plan B (rain) and Plan C (heat, wind, storm) for every day, each naming what it replaces.
  - [ ] Clock times on the timeline, so the Calendar can offer Add for each item.
  - [ ] Everything to book ahead (tables, classes, salons, tickets, transport, a booking to cancel) as a
        Before we go item, with its book-by date and why that date, linked to its stop or meal.
  - [ ] A forecast place for every base and its dates.
- **Gallery**
  - [ ] Official accounts' posts that show the place and match the page's words, each once a day.
- **Holidays**
  - [ ] Public holidays on the trip dates and what they change (shops, venues, surcharges, transport),
        as an alert chip on the opener and in the day's Shop the day.

## Road trip and countryside (complete, proven on v15)

- **Shape**
  - [ ] Bases chosen so no day starts with a long drive after a flight; one or two bases rather than
        a new bed every night (v15: two bases, the second in the middle of the trip).
  - [ ] Check-in and check-out times for every base; the arrival day and the departure day kept free of long outings.
  - [ ] One principal outing a day, back at the base by mid-afternoon, rest before dinner.
  - [ ] Transfer days are transfer days: one real midway break (toilets, refreshments, a stretch), not sightseeing.
- **Driving**
  - [ ] Road distance and model drive time for every leg (a routing service, cited), and planning
        times with buffers for parking, traffic and holiday crowds. Say which is which.
  - [ ] Total driving per day stated; a midway break on any leg over about two hours.
  - [ ] No dusk or night driving on country roads where wildlife is active; return times set to suit.
  - [ ] Fuel: refill when convenient, not on the last stretch.
  - [ ] Rental car checklist: automatic, room for every traveller and all luggage, driver
        eligibility, insurance excess, extra driver, fuel policy, mileage, depot hours, airport return.
  - [ ] A "nobody wants to drive" alternative: a private driver or tour, quoted in full before changing the plan.
  - [ ] Taxi and rideshare: do not assume they are available for regional returns; book ahead.
  - [ ] Fare estimates from the official tariffs, with the method shown; "estimate, not a quote".
- **Country towns**
  - [ ] Shop and supermarket trading hours outside the city, including Sunday and public-holiday
        closures; groceries bought before arriving where needed.
  - [ ] Cafés and bakeries with their service hours; tastings are a treat, not lunch.
  - [ ] A replacement stop for each optional town ("swap, don't stack").
- **Safety in each area**
  - [ ] Nearest 24-hour emergency department, with road distance and time.
  - [ ] Pharmacy, a GP, and police: the nearest station, its counter hours, and the nearest one open 24/7.
  - [ ] National emergency number, a health advice line, a wildlife or roadside line.
- **Calendar and weather**
  - [ ] Long-term climate averages per base (day maximum, night minimum, rain days): context, not a forecast.
  - [ ] Heat precautions for older travellers: outdoor stops early, shade, air-conditioned breaks,
        medicines out of a hot car, the clinician's advice before departure.
- **Stays**
  - [ ] Room layout per traveller (real beds, no sofa-bed assumption), quiet unit, step-free or ground
        floor, shower type, air-conditioning per bedroom, parking.
  - [ ] Places to avoid, with the reason and a source (building works, noise, stairs).
  - [ ] Dress codes and evening rules at hotel restaurants, where the venue states them.
  - [ ] A ready-to-send quote request, clearly marked "nothing has been sent".

## City and culture

- [ ] Museums and galleries: days closed, late openings, timed tickets, free days, cloakroom and bag rules.
- [ ] Public transport: the pass or card to use, airport link, step-free stations, last services.
- [ ] Neighbourhoods by mood (old town, design district, markets) with a walking radius per day.
- [ ] Food halls, markets and signature dishes by neighbourhood, for Taste & Shop.
- [ ] Performances and events on the dates: venue, dress, start times, booking window.
- [ ] Restaurant reservations: release dates, dress codes, tasting menu lengths.
- [ ] Rainy-day Plan B (covered markets, galleries); heat Plan C (shaded parks, river or garden cafés).
- [ ] Pickpocket hot spots and safe late-evening routes back to the hotel.

## Nature and wildlife

- [ ] Park entry, permits and timed access; ranger-led options.
- [ ] Best hours for wildlife; seasonal closures, breeding seasons and rules on distance.
- [ ] Trail facts: length, height gain, surface, shade, toilets, and a short version.
- [ ] Tide, sunrise and sunset times where they matter.
- [ ] Fire danger, flood and heat warnings: where to check them, and the Plan C that avoids them.
- [ ] Mobile coverage and the nearest help on remote stretches; what to carry (water, layers).
- [ ] Responsible-viewing operators only; how they are accredited.

## Food and wine

- [ ] Producers, farm shops and markets with their tasting hours and booking rules.
- [ ] A designated driver or a driver service; never tasting and driving.
- [ ] Signature dishes and where to have them; seasonal produce on the dates.
- [ ] Dietary needs per traveller and which venues can meet them.
- [ ] Shipping produce home: who ships, to where, and the import limits.
- [ ] Lunch is a meal: tastings do not replace it.

## Cruise

- [ ] Ship, cabin and deck plan; embarkation and disembarkation times and terminal.
- [ ] Each port: time ashore, all-aboard time, tender or pier, the distance to the sights.
- [ ] Shore days planned to return with a wide margin; the ship will not wait.
- [ ] Each port's tastes and shopping for Taste & Shop, with the hours on the day in port.
- [ ] Sea-day programme highlights; dress codes for formal nights.
- [ ] Sea conditions, seasickness advice, and which ports get skipped in bad weather (Plan B).
- [ ] Accessibility on board and ashore (tenders often are not step-free).

## Faith and heritage

- [ ] Opening times around services; when visits are not allowed.
- [ ] Dress codes (shoulders, knees, head covering, shoes), photography rules, quiet zones.
- [ ] Religious calendar on the dates: festivals, fasting, closures, crowds.
- [ ] Guided tours by recognised guides; the story of each place in two or three lines.
- [ ] Steps, kneeling and long standing: seats and step-free routes.
- [ ] Offerings, donations and etiquette, said respectfully.

## Young family

- [ ] Naps, meals and early nights shape each day; one big thing a day.
- [ ] Toilets, baby change, shade and play areas at each stop (suitability marks on every stop).
- [ ] Pram or carrier per stop; lifts and step-free routes.
- [ ] Child prices and ages, car seats in taxis and rental cars, cots and family rooms.
- [ ] A rain Plan B that children enjoy (aquarium, museum with hands-on rooms).
- [ ] Sun and water safety: patrolled beaches, swim between the flags, hats and sunscreen.
- [ ] The nearest children's emergency department.

## Gentle travel for seniors

- [ ] Short walks, seats at every stop, lifts or ramps, step-free rooms with walk-in showers.
- [ ] Door-to-door transport for anything beyond a short walk.
- [ ] Late starts, a rest every afternoon, early dinners, no back-to-back long days.
- [ ] Heat Plan C as a rule, not an exception; regular medicines in hand luggage; the clinician's advice on travel and heat.
- [ ] Travel insurance that covers declared conditions.
- [ ] Large-print friendly: the print mode, phone numbers as buttons, the concierge panel per area.

## Celebration

- [ ] The occasion's moment: the place, the time (golden hour, sunset), the table, the cake or the
      flowers, and who arranges each.
- [ ] Private options as upgrades: a private dining room, a chef's table, a yacht, a photographer, a driver.
- [ ] Book-ahead windows and deposits; what happens if the weather turns (Plan B for the moment itself).
- [ ] Surprise handling: what the guests must not see on the page.
- [ ] Dress code for each event; a short programme for the day.
